# Daily Paper

An offline study collection for learning influential AI, machine-learning, and deep-learning papers through active lessons.

Open [the lesson library](index.html) in a browser, choose a paper, and work through its explanation, diagrams, prompts, and checks at your own pace. Paper titles open the original PDFs; **Read the lesson** opens the guided HTML lesson.

## What each lesson includes

- A plain-language bridge into the paper’s central idea
- A gradual one-hour route with analogies and prerequisite support
- Careful explanations of the notation and method
- Worked examples, original-paper evidence, and active-recall questions with answers
- A link to the original paper for deeper reading

Lesson folders use stable title-based names without date prefixes. Dates remain in `curriculum.json` so the library can preserve study history and sort newest first. Run `node scripts/build_catalog.mjs` after changing curriculum records.
